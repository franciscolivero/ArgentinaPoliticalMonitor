Sources

  • Pollsters’ own releases: every Cadem and Criteria wave (Chile), read from the PDFs they publish; CID Gallup’s weekly bulletins (Central America); Mitofsky’s monthly ranking of leaders’ approval.
  • Wikipedia polling tables for Mexico and El Salvador.
  • Google News, searched daily in each country’s edition and by pollster name. A poll is kept when the headline names the sitting president and gives an approval share; headlines that do not name the pollster count at half weight. Regional rankings are read too: CB Consultora (positive image), Mitofsky’s ranking of leaders’ approval and AtlasIntel’s Latam Pulse. Press reports are usually placed four days before publication.
  • Average: Gaussian-kernel poll average with a 21-day bandwidth; a pollster publishing several times a month counts as one voice in that month.
  • Argentina: the monitor’s own approval average (Milei approval).
